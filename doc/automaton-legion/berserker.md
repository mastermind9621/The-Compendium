# Berserker

**Classification:** Medium
**Threat level:** Medium to High

---

## Specifications:
Berserkers are the lightly-armoured chainsaw maniacs of the legion, hell-bent on rushing down any liberated individual heedless of their own continued functionality. They use their lack of armour to their advantantage, charging chainsaws up with ferocious speed. Each of their two arm-saws are built for maximum pain and suffering, ensuring that whomever of our brave freedom fighters survives the attack, is left with an injury from their brutish butchery.

## Weaknesses:
As with all medium-classed automatons, their main weakness are their central control units located at the upper section of their torso between their two arms. Additionally, their lack of proper armour renders their whole body vulnerable to light pen and up. They have a glowing red weakness around their midsection where the torso connects with its locomotive systems.

## Recommended strategies:
Awareness is key when engaged. A group of these is a threat alone, but stay calm, take note of their position and be aware of their attack range as well as your own. Their single-minded hatred toward humankind can be used against them, by luring them into traps laid out by the brilliant strategic minds of the Helldivers.
